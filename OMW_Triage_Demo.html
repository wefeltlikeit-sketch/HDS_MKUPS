The Framework

Three questions, asked together, at the moment a fracture appears.

Every ADT feed and inpatient authorization runs through a three-component score. Each component is independently calibrated, independently interpretable, and independently retrainable as your outcome data accumulates.

Front 01
FCS

Fracture Confirmation

Given this signal, is this actually a HEDIS-qualifying fragility fracture?

Filters ED recoding, non-qualifying sites, and high-trauma events before they reach the outreach pipeline.

Front 02
EDRS

Exclusion Development Risk

Will this member acquire HEDIS exclusions before December 31?

Anchored on a claims-based frailty instrument (HFRS or Kim CFI), layered with dementia meds, hospice signals, and utilization trajectory.

Front 03
NCRS

Non-Compliance Risk

If denominator-eligible, will they miss the BMD or therapy?

Cross-medication PDC is the strongest single predictor — a member adherent to a statin is adherent to a bisphosphonate.

Opportunity=FCS×(1 − EDRS)×(1 − NCRS)

to targeted intervention.

The economic case is straightforward. Every provider message has a cost — in attention, in alert fatigue, in eroded trust in your quality program. Every missed numerator has a cost — in Star Ratings revenue, in secondary fracture risk, in actual elderly women who needed treatment and didn't get it.

The three-component model doesn't eliminate either cost. It assigns them intelligently. High-confidence cases get full intervention immediately. Ambiguous cases mature. Low-confidence cases get monitored without noise.
